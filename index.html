Privacy Policy for VESCWatcher

Effective Date: February 2025

Summary: VESCWatcher is a standalone Wear OS application designed to connect locally to your VESC-based Onewheel electric board via Bluetooth LE. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or location information.

1. Information We Process

VESCWatcher operates strictly locally on your Wear OS smartwatch:

  • VESC Telemetry Data: The application reads real-time speed, duty cycle, battery voltage, motor/FET temperatures, and footpad sensor status from your VESC board via Bluetooth LE. This data is processed temporarily in memory to display on your screen and is never saved, stored, or transmitted over the internet.

2. Device Permissions Used

To function properly, VESCWatcher requests the following Android device permissions:

  • Bluetooth Permissions (BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT): Used exclusively to discover and maintain a local wireless connection with your VESC board.
  • Location Permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): Android requires Location permission to scan for nearby Bluetooth LE devices. VESCWatcher does not track, record, store, or share your physical location.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE): Used to keep the local Bluetooth LE connection active when your watch screen enters low-power sleep mode.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

VESCWatcher does not contain any third-party advertising frameworks, tracking tools, analytics SDKs, or external data collection services.

4. Data Security

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, your privacy is fully preserved on your device.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
